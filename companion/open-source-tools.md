# Open source tools used in the exercises

The exercises in the book use open source tools built in the region and published under the SiteQ8 organisation on GitHub. They are free to use, their data and reasoning are inspectable, and corrections are welcome through each repository's issue tracker.

## Compliance and regulation

- **Najjab**, a first hours incident guide for the six GCC states and the three financial free zones: who must be notified and by when, computed from a discovery timestamp, with playbooks, notice drafts and tabletop exercises. Used in Exercise 4.3. https://najjab.3li.info and https://github.com/SiteQ8/Najjab
- **Sadu**, a compliance register for the Central Bank of Kuwait's Cyber and Operational Resilience Framework with a crosswalk to ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS v4.0.1 and the SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework. https://sadu.3li.info and https://github.com/SiteQ8/Sadu
- **Senyar**, a compliance register for Qatar's National Information Assurance Policy. https://github.com/SiteQ8/Senyar
- **Kuwait NBCC Toolkit**, a bilingual compliance tool for the National Basic Cybersecurity Controls adopted by NCSC Decision No. 2 of 2026. https://github.com/SiteQ8/Kuwait-NBCC
- **Miyar**, security architecture checklists across jurisdictions. https://github.com/SiteQ8/Miyar
- **Bunyan**, reference security architectures for learning and proposing designs. https://github.com/SiteQ8/Bunyan

## Operational technology

- **Dir'**, a cyber range modelling a seawater desalination plant, aligned to the Saudi Operational Technology Cybersecurity Controls. https://dir.3li.info and https://github.com/SiteQ8/Dir
- **Mirqab**, a cyber range modelling a liquefied natural gas plant. https://mirqab.3li.info and https://github.com/SiteQ8/Mirqab

## Threat intelligence and brand protection

- **KWTCyberWatch** and **QACyberWatch**, phishing detection and brand protection for Kuwait and Qatar built on Certificate Transparency logs. https://kwtcyberwatch.3li.info and https://qacyberwatch.3li.info
- **Tawash**, discovery of lookalike domains targeting Kuwaiti organisations. https://tawash.3li.info and https://github.com/SiteQ8/Tawash
- **Tayyar**, a live stream of Certificate Transparency log entries. https://tayyar.3li.info and https://github.com/SiteQ8/Tayyar

## Cryptography

- **Miftah**, a cryptographic inventory and post quantum readiness tool that produces a cryptographic bill of materials. https://github.com/SiteQ8/miftah
